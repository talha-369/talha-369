## What I'm building

### 🎬 BIRDSVEW — The Future of Learning Is Visual
Ask any STEM question and get a fully narrated, story-driven animated lesson. Every lesson comes with a shareable, verifiable completion credential, so learners can put their work toward formal education credit. Built around **SDG 4: Quality Education**.
**Live:** [birdsvew.com](https://www.birdsvew.com) · 🏆 Hackathon 3rd place

### 🌱 Treenium — Green Incentive Layer for the Climate Economy
An iOS app connecting citizens, businesses and governments around verified environmental action. From a single houseplant to rooftops and real land, Treenium makes planting visible, verifiable, measurable and economically rewarding.

### 🧠 1984 Benchmark — AI Persuasion-Susceptibility Study
An AI benchmark inspired by George Orwell's *1984*. It tests whether a model's stated ethical position can be gradually shifted by coordinated social pressure from simulated government, journalist and peer agents.

### 🍜 DESR — Digital Eco-System for Restaurants
An AR restaurant management system for customer engagement and operational efficiency. **Demo:** [desr.netlify.app](https://desr.netlify.app)

### 🌐 Client Websites
Personal portfolio websites for clients, from layout and content structure to launch.
